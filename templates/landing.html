Live object detection

The camera feed is analysed frame by frame. Every object gets a bounding box, a label and a confidence score.

Buzzer and alerts

Anything outside your allowed list triggers the buzzer, a snapshot and a dashboard notice.

Ask Mini-Bot

Say "find bottle" or "what do you see". Mini-Bot looks through its camera and answers out loud.

Human verification

Confirm or reject each alert. The dashboard turns your answers into an accuracy score.
